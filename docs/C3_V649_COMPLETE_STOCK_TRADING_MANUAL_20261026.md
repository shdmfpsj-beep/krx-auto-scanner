## Mandatory decision and report gates

1. At top include symbol, market, **as-of timestamp/time zone**, original report cutoff, delayed / close / official KRX 09:00–15:30 provenance. Plan freshness must be checked against latest dated stock observation; current quoted price below old invalidation floor => `PAST_PLAN_INVALIDATED`; otherwise old plan day is past => `REFRESH_REQUIRED`. Under either status **do not recycle historical A/B/C buy bands or historical targets as CURRENT**. Always separate investor news timestamps from price timestamps.
2. Separate `OBSERVED`: price, confirmed dated trend structure, actual named peer flows with verification link; `INFERENCE`: judged support/resistance from chart, forward triggers; `UNVERIFIED`: casual claims of foreign/institutional buying, exact volume-by-price POC/VAH/VAL without lower timeframe trades, projected targets without previous price or issuer evidence, vague highest-conviction labels. No fake rise probabilities.
3. Technical hierarchy: market and sector state, company quality/catalyst, weekly/daily price structure, completed structural stop BEFORE entry, nearest observable overhead resistance, first realistically contactable buy zone, optionally better support and conditional breakout/retest, conditional 1st/2nd targets. No arbitrary -10% second buy, no fabricated cheaper bids to create RR>2, no fixed account-risk to move stop. Breakout must be a completed qualified session with documented volume and timestamp, not predicted 15-min hold.
4. Separate **new entrant** action from **existing holder**, and strong breakout / failed breakout / confirmed support / support-loss scenarios. Current target/entry fields explicitly `DATA_REQUIRED` when latest as-of price and source are insufficient. The third confidence field can be high/ordinary/low/defer with evidence count and override events; not numerical probability without calibration.
5. A/B Work v6.38 and price independent v6.46/48 stay frozen historical research, no new profitability from this report automation. No broker orders or K100 changes. PR #25 stays draft.
